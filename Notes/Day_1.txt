2. TravelBytes AI kya hai?

TravelBytes AI ek AI-powered dynamic tourism planning assistant hai.

Normal travel planner generally:

User Input
     ↓
Recommendations
     ↓
Itinerary

TravelBytes AI ka final goal:

User Input
     ↓
AI Recommendation
     ↓
Itinerary
     ↓
Real-Time Conditions
     ↓
Weather / Traffic / Closure
     ↓
AI Re-planning
     ↓
Updated Itinerary

Iska main USP hai:

TravelBytes AI doesn't just plan the trip — it continuously adapts the trip.

14. Interest Score

Function:

calculate_interest_score(place, interests)

User ke interests:

heritage, food, culture

ko split kiya jata hai.

user_interests = [
    interest.strip().lower()
    for interest in interests.split(",")
]

Place ke tags bhi split hote hain:

place_tags = [
    tag.strip().lower()
    for tag in place["tags"].split(",")
]

Phir matching interests count hote hain.

Example:

User:
heritage, food, culture

Place:
heritage, history, photography

Matching:

heritage = match
food = no
culture = no

Therefore:

Interest Score = 1/3
15. Budget Score

Function:

calculate_budget_score(place, budget)

Ye check karta hai ki place ka estimated cost user ke budget ke comparison mein kaisa hai.

Logic:

Low cost
   ↓
High score

High cost
   ↓
Lower score

Example:

Budget = ₹8000
Place Cost = ₹50

To place ko high budget-fit score milega.

16. Rating Score

Function:

calculate_rating_score(place)

Rating ko 0–1 scale mein convert kiya:

return place["rating"] / 5

Example:

Rating = 4.5

4.5 / 5 = 0.90

Therefore:

Rating Score = 0.90
17. Final AI Score

Final score:

final_score = (
    interest_score * 0.50 +
    budget_score * 0.30 +
    rating_score * 0.20
)
Weightage
Factor	Weight
Interest	50%
Budget	30%
Rating	20%
Why these weights?

Day 1 MVP mein humne user preference ko highest priority di.

Interest → 50%
Budget   → 30%
Rating   → 20%

Isliye recommendation user-centric hai.

18. Places Ranking

recommend_places(

place["score"] = score

Then places descending order mein sort hote hain:

recommended_places.sort(

Meaning:

      ↓
      2nd
